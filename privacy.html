Privacy Policy

Nemik rclone is a private application used by its owner to access Google Drive through rclone.

The application accesses Google Drive data only for operations explicitly performed by the owner through rclone.

Google OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's systems and are not sold, shared, or provided to third parties.

Google user data is not independently collected, analyzed, sold, or used for advertising.

Authorization may be revoked at any time through the user's Google Account permissions.
